About Classic Edge Atelier

Dedicated Indian craftsmanship, verified leather sourcing, and transparent wholesale manufacturing for international trade.

“Classic Edge Atelier is an India-based manufacturer and supplier of leather products and textiles, engineered to serve the high standards of international businesses seeking wholesale supply, custom branding, and long-term supply partnerships.”

Established with a dedication to artisanal perfection, our workshop brings together centuries-old Indian leatherworking traditions with modern export assembly. We specialize in formal dress shoes, traditional Gulf slippers, luxury wallets, business briefcases, solid leather belts, and woven textiles.

Our primary clients are importers, wholesalers, distributors, retail chains, and private-label brands across the United Arab Emirates (Dubai, Abu Dhabi), Saudi Arabia (Riyadh, Jeddah), Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, and Oman. We recognize that international buyers require strict consistency in leather grade, durable stitching, accurate carton packaging, and fast, respectful communication.

Authentic Leather Grades

We work with select full-grain calfskin, drum-dyed goat and buffalo leathers, and supple suede. Every batch undergoes rigorous inspection for uniform thickness, tensile strength, and color fastness.

Master Carton Packaging

Footwear and accessories are packed in reinforced, export-standard cartons designed to withstand maritime humidity and long-distance cargo handling without crushing or deformation.

Reliable Trade Compliance

We provide full export documentation including Commercial Invoices, Packing Lists with weight and volume metrics, and Certificates of Origin for streamlined customs clearance across GCC ports.
